What We Do Not Collect

Across all three apps, we never collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials
  • Your precise GPS location or IP address
  • Camera, microphone, or photo library data
  • Contacts, call logs, or SMS
  • Payment card or banking information (all payments go through Google Play)
  • Any content you write, save, or type in the apps
None of our apps require account creation. There is no sign-in, no profile synced to our servers, and no cloud backup of your personal data.

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR — EEA & UK Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the following legal bases apply to how data is processed through our apps:

  • Legitimate interests — Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are used to improve app performance and fix crashes. This processing is in the legitimate interest of providing a stable, improving product.
  • Consent — AdMob personalised advertising relies on your consent via your device's ad personalisation setting. You may withdraw consent at any time by opting out of personalised ads in your Android settings.
  • Contract performance — Google Play Billing data is processed to fulfil your purchase of the Platinum unlock.
EEA / UK users: If you believe your data is being processed unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority (e.g. ICO in the UK, or your EU Member State's supervisory authority).

Data Retention & Deletion

Local app data (WordNerd): All study progress, streaks, bookmarks, and notes are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data. We cannot recover it once deleted.

Firebase data: Crash reports and analytics events are retained by Google per their standard data retention policies (typically 90 days for Crashlytics, configurable for Analytics). This data is not linked to any identifiable user on our end.

AdMob data: Ad impression and interaction data is managed by Google per their advertising data policies.

Payment data: We retain no payment records. All transaction history is managed by Google Play.

Security

Because we do not transmit personal data to our own servers, the attack surface is minimal. Local data in WordNerd (progress, notes, preferences) is stored in the app's private sandboxed storage on your device, inaccessible to other apps.

For data processed by Firebase and AdMob, Google's enterprise-grade security infrastructure applies. No electronic system is 100% secure, but we have taken reasonable steps to minimise risk within our control.

California Residents (CCPA)

California residents have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale of personal information.

We do not sell personal information. For any CCPA-related requests, contact us at kreedo.tech@gmail.com.

11

Do-Not-Track & Advertising Controls

Our apps do not respond to browser-level Do-Not-Track (DNT) signals. However, you can control ad-related tracking at the device level:

  • Android 12 and above: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete Advertising ID
  • Android 11 and below: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation

Opting out means AdMob will serve non-personalised ads rather than no ads. Purchasing Platinum in WordNerd removes all ads entirely.
